About InterviewForge

Why we built this

Most people bomb interviews not because they aren't qualified, but because they never practiced out loud under pressure. InterviewForge exists to fix that: realistic questions by role, a real timer, and STAR coaching — so the interview feels like your tenth rep, not your first.

How it works

Pick one of 8 roles, answer timed questions, review what interviewers actually look for, and check your answers against the STAR framework. Run full 5-question mock interviews and track your average score and daily streak over time.

Privacy first

Everything runs in your browser. Your answers and progress are stored on your device — we never see, store, or sell your data. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Pricing

The core practice tool is free forever: 5 questions a day, no account needed. If you're actively interviewing and want unlimited practice and mock interviews, Pro is $15/month or $119/year. That's the whole business model — no ads, no selling data, no dark patterns.
