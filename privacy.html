Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Short version

InterviewForge runs entirely in your browser. Your answers, scores, and progress are stored on your own device. We don't see, store, or sell your data.

What we collect

  • Practice data: your answers, self-scores, and progress are saved to your browser's local storage. This never leaves your device.
  • Contact messages: if you email us, we receive your name, email address, and message so we can reply.
  • Payments: Pro subscriptions are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card details; Stripe's own privacy policy applies to payment data.

Cookies

We don't set tracking cookies. Your browser's local storage is used only to remember your practice progress on your device.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your rights

You can clear your practice data at any time by clearing your browser's site data. To request deletion of any emails you've sent us, contact support@interviewforge.example.com.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and post the new version on this page.
